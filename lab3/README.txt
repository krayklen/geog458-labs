The Purpose of the map in 3-2 was to show where bike racks are located across the city
and show which areas are lacking so that those areas could be improved
The bike rack data came from Seattle's Geo Database https://data-seattlecitygis.opendata.arcgis.com/datasets/e82f927b630f4fb8b4551baffa437370_0/explore?location=47.659809%2C-122.305966%2C15
This makes the study area in Seattle 
The basemap is made using MapBox Studio
The map uses light-green to dark green to match the common color used to represent
bike lanes
The map can zoom from 8-12 tile range
